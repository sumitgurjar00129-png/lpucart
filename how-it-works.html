FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 4-digit OTP handover work?

When you complete your order on LPUcart, the system generates a random 4-digit OTP (e.g. 5821). When the delivery partner reaches your hostel or room, they must ask you for this OTP and enter it into their dashboard. If the OTP is incorrect, the delivery partner cannot mark the order as delivered. This guarantees your package is never taken by someone else.

How does the Live Location delivery work?

If you are not in your hostel—for instance, if you are studying in the Central Library, eating at the food court, or sitting on the university lawns—select "Live Location". With your browser's permission, LPUcart fetches your GPS coordinates and creates a Google Maps pin. The runner navigates directly to your pin for a flat ₹19 fee.

What are the operational time slots?

LPUcart runs four daily delivery batches: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM - 02:00 PM, 04:00 PM - 06:00 PM, and 06:00 PM - 08:00 PM. Orders placed in advance are batched and dispatched promptly within your chosen slot.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes! Both instant UPI QR payment and Cash on Delivery (COD) are supported. You can scan the UPI QR code on the order page or hand over the exact cash to the delivery partner upon arrival.
